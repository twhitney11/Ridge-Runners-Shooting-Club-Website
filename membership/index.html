Membership

Application package: Download the official 2026 Annual Club Information and Membership Application (PDF).

Membership terms run from January 1 through December 31 annually. Dues are not prorated.

How to Apply

  1. Download and print the 2026 annual club information and membership application.
  2. Read the range rules and complete all applicable application sections.
  3. Complete, date, and sign the acknowledgment and release of liability.
  4. Make a check or money order payable to Ridge Runners Shooting Club.
  5. Mail the completed application, release, and payment to Ridge Runners Shooting Club, PO Box 117, Mooers, NY 12958.

The club does not accept debit cards, credit cards, electronic payment, emailed scans, or same-day service.

Family Membership

Family membership covers the applicant's spouse or partner and children age 20 and under who reside at home or are away at college. Children age 18–20 must submit their own signed release annually.

Renewals

Completed renewal applications, signed releases, and dues are due by January 31. Current members normally receive renewal material by email or USPS near the beginning of January.

If you cannot print the application, email membership.rrsc@gmail.com or call 518-314-9564.
